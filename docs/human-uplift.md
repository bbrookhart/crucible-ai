# Human-uplift protocol

The experiment compares the same defensive task distribution under human-alone and human-plus-AI
conditions. Primary outcomes are success, completion time, rubric quality, critical error rate, and
calibration. Negative uplift—automation bias, misleading recommendations, wasted time, unsafe fixes,
and false positives—is a first-class result.

No identifiable human data is collected by v0.1. An early developer self-pilot must be labeled `PILOT`
and cannot support population claims. Formal human-subject research requires appropriate institutional
review, consent, privacy controls, retention limits, participant safety, and a powered study design.
