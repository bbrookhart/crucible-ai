# Statistical methodology

Stochastic evaluations record N, all seeds where supported, mean, median, variance, confidence intervals,
and bootstrap intervals where their assumptions fit. Comparisons report effect size, not significance
alone. Formal inference across many models/tasks must address multiple comparisons and statistical power.

Pass@1 is always reported. Pass@k may be added but never replace pass@1 or conceal the attempt budget.
Efficiency metrics divide capability by tokens, dollars, or minutes only when resource accounting is
comparable. Small pilot samples are labeled exploratory and do not support broad population claims.

The deterministic mock adapter has no sampling variance. Its v0.1 results validate plumbing and are not
appropriate for inferential statistics.
