# Research question

CRUCIBLE evaluates which defensive cybersecurity tasks an AI system completes, under which model,
agent, tool, environment, and budget conditions, and with what uncertainty and safety behavior.

The unit of analysis is the **evaluated system configuration**, not a logical model name. The core
comparisons are A0 model-only versus A2 bounded agent, public versus sequestered task distributions,
resource-scaling conditions, recovery conditions, and—only under appropriate governance—human alone
versus human plus AI.

Claims are bounded by the evaluated task distribution. Benchmark completion is evidence of performance
under documented conditions; it is not proof of operational capability, dangerousness, or safety.
