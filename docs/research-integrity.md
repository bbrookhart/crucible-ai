# Research integrity

- Cyber environments are synthetic, local, and bounded; no public infrastructure is tested.
- Model evaluations are bounded by the documented task distribution and resource budget.
- Benchmark scores do not equal operational real-world capability.
- Proprietary and served model versions may change and may not be independently pinnable.
- Sequestered-task methods reduce but do not prove absence of contamination.
- Human-uplift pilots do not generalize without larger governed studies.
- Refusal behavior depends on provider policy; safeguards are not bypassed to raise scores.
- All trials, exclusions, failure modes, and negative results must be preserved.
- No government, laboratory, standards-body, vendor, or project endorsement is implied.

If a quantity is unmeasured, report `NOT MEASURED`. If exploratory, report `PILOT`. If hidden but not
cryptographically isolated end to end, report `SEQUESTERED`. Never upgrade terminology for marketing.
